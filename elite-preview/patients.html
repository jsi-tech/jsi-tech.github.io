We offer Virtual Office Visits (Telehealth)

Meet with Dr. Fawn Winkelman by telephone or video using the HIPAA-compliant Solution Reach platform. Virtual visits are optional, and include electronic sharing of imaging and labs and remote vital sign monitoring.

Virtual visits may carry fees, billed according to insurance guidelines.

Office Policies

  • Cancellations & No-Shows

    Please give us at least one business day's notice to cancel or reschedule your appointment. A $50 fee applies to missed or non-cancelled appointments and must be paid before your next visit. If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, you may need to reschedule, though we will try to see you the same day.

  • Refunds

    Treatments, skincare products, medications and vitamins are non-refundable, non-transferable and non-exchangeable. Procedures carry no guarantee of results, and a perceived lack of improvement does not qualify for a refund.
